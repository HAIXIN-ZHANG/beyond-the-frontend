## Each future reading page: two gates, separately assigned

1. **Scope:** Coordinator/user chooses the specific page/problem, dependencies, allowed paths, review IDs, checks and next boundary. The complete v1 outline is not an Assigned batch.
2. **Analysis:** Writer researches primary/official claims, sets source/date/conditions, detailed sections, original examples/diagrams and validation plans. Identify reuse versus expansion of any accepted source.
3. **Analysis review:** freeze and hand off; Coordinator reads actual material, returns findings and explicitly accepts the analysis plus writing scope. Owner revises/re-submits. No full prose before this gate.
4. **Manuscript:** write only the accepted scope with connected explanation, necessary examples, boundaries and source links. Label assumptions and real checks; substantive mechanism/scope changes return to analysis.
5. **Manuscript review:** submit a distinct revision/review gate, with actual document/code/diagram checks, open issues and differences from analysis. Owner handles feedback; analysis approval is not prose approval.
6. **Integration/publication:** Coordinator handles Git/exact-head CI/merge under authorization. Publication and content-allowlist changes need their own accepted source/scope; merge is not deployment.

Use the [stable Writer guide](../../docs/writer-sync-prompt.md) for startup/recovery and the [existing handoff template](../../docs/handoffs/TEMPLATE.md) for the task brief/evidence. No new per-page planning files until a page is assigned. Keep drafts, review decisions, merged revisions and deployed content separate.

## Manuscript acceptance criteria

- Readers can explain a concrete user/product question through a correct mechanism, an original example and conditional choices, not just a term list.
- Necessary assumptions/prerequisites are supplied or linked to the main topic home; optional depth is skippable for Junior readers while useful to experienced readers.
- Normal/failure/unknown outcomes, security/accessibility/testing/observability/performance appear where they affect the example, not merely as a final checklist.
- Version-sensitive behavior is checked against relevant official sources; experiments carry environment/conditions and unrun checks are explicit. No fictional source, scale, employer incident, quality or productivity claim.
- Diagrams/tables/code preserve technical meaning, state arrow/label assumptions and have usable text explanations. Test only what is meaningful and within authorized tools/scope.
- AI product cases distinguish model output, retrieved evidence, business authority, user controls and task-quality evals. Coding-AI cases retain relevant inputs, real diffs/checks and human decisions without private transcripts.
- Cross-chapter reuse follows the outline's topic homes; avoid duplicating definitions or a second canonical manuscript.
- Handoff names exact revision/files/evidence/open issues and freezes them for actual review. Changed work does not inherit old acceptance.
